Welcome to Glow and Grow

At Glow and Grow, we are dedicated to empowering children to reach their full potential through the teachings of the Kingdom of God. Our programs and events are designed to inspire and guide young minds, helping them develop strong values, confidence, and a passion for making a positive impact in the world. Join us on this transformative journey as we nurture the leaders of tomorrow and create a brighter future for our community.

Igniting Potential, Nurturing Growth

Our mission is to nurture children to shine brightly and grow into their best selves. Join us in cultivating a generation of confident, compassionate, and capable individuals. Join us on this transformative journey as we empower young minds to reach their full potential.

Empowering Young Minds for a Brighter Future.

Through our programs, we inspire children to discover their unique gifts and talents. Join us in creating an environment where every child is encouraged to grow, learn, and shine.

Career Outcomes

Wondering what you can do with a Children's, Youth, and Family Ministry degree?.

Our online master's degree in children's, youth, and family ministry program will prepare you to invest in the future of the church by serving the church of today—its children!

Skills

Learn how to:

  • Cultivate a philosophy of ministry to children and families so you can meet real needs with biblical solutions
  • Be a successful member of any ministry team through developing your leadership skills
  • Lead children’s and family ministry in your church, your community, and the global arena by gaining intercultural competence
  • Take practical courses with concepts and strategies you can apply to your current ministry experience

Career

Our graduates are prepared for a number of roles, including:

  • Children’s pastor
  • Family and Youth Coordinator
  • Children’s, Youth, and Family Minister
  • International children ministry
  • Leadership role in nonprofits serving children and families
